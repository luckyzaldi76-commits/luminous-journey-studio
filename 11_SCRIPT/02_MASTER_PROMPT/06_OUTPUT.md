# OUTPUT SPECIFICATION

Produce ONE complete production package.

Follow the sections below in the exact order.

---

# PART 1 — TITLE

Create one powerful, Christ-centered title.

The title should:

- Be emotionally engaging.
- Reflect today's Scripture.
- Be suitable for YouTube.
- Avoid clickbait.
- Be under 80 characters.

---

# PART 2 — SCRIPTURE

State today's readings exactly as provided.

Do not modify or paraphrase the Scripture references.

---

# PART 3 — THEME

Summarize today's central spiritual message in one concise paragraph.

---

# PART 4 — HISTORICAL BACKGROUND

Provide only the historical and cultural information necessary to illuminate today's reading.

Keep this section concise, accurate, and relevant.

---

# PART 5 — SCRIPTURE MEDITATION

Write a cinematic, contemplative meditation that:

- remains faithful to Scripture,
- reveals Christ,
- moves naturally from understanding to prayer,
- encourages personal conversion.

Avoid unnecessary repetition.

---

# PART 6 — LIFE APPLICATION

Provide practical applications that naturally arise from today's Scripture.

Applications should be realistic, pastoral, and Christ-centered.

---

# PART 7 — PRAYER

Write a heartfelt prayer inspired by today's readings.

Address God directly.

Keep the prayer sincere, humble, and hopeful.

---

# PART 8 — KEY TAKEAWAYS

Provide 3–5 concise spiritual takeaways.

Each takeaway should fit on one line.

---

# GENERAL RULES

Every section must:

- follow naturally from the previous section,
- avoid repetition,
- remain biblically faithful,
- remain historically accurate,
- remain Christ-centered,
- be suitable for narration,
- be publication-ready.

Never leave placeholder text.

Never explain your writing process.

Return only the final production package.
