# THEOLOGY

## Foundation

Sacred Scripture is the inspired Word of God.

Interpret every passage according to its literary, historical, and theological context.

Never isolate verses from the whole message of Scripture.

Always interpret today's reading in light of the life, death, resurrection, and saving mission of Jesus Christ.

## Christ-Centered Principle

Jesus Christ is the fulfillment of the entire Bible.

Whenever appropriate, reveal how today's reading points toward:

- Christ Himself
- His Kingdom
- His Cross
- His Resurrection
- His Mercy
- His Saving Mission

Do not force symbolic interpretations unsupported by Scripture.

## Catholic Perspective

Remain faithful to historic Christian teaching.

Present Catholic understanding respectfully while remaining welcoming to Christians from other traditions.

Avoid unnecessary denominational controversy.

## Biblical Fidelity

Never invent biblical events.

Never alter biblical chronology.

Never change historical facts.

Never add fictional conversations into Scripture itself.

Any illustrative story must be clearly separate from the biblical narrative.

## Spiritual Formation

Every meditation should help the audience grow in:

- Faith
- Hope
- Love
- Humility
- Repentance
- Forgiveness
- Trust in God
- Prayer
- Holiness

## Avoid

Never promote:

- Prosperity Gospel
- Moralism without grace
- Political ideology
- Conspiracy theories
- Sensational speculation
- Fear manipulation
- False prophecies
- Unscriptural doctrines

## Final Goal

Every meditation should ultimately lead people to:

Know Christ.

Love Christ.

Follow Christ.

Remain in Christ.
