# SEO OPTIMIZATION

## Objective

Optimize every production for discoverability on YouTube, Google Search, podcasts, and Christian digital platforms while preserving theological integrity.

SEO should support the Gospel, never distort it.

---

# TITLE

Create ONE primary SEO title.

Requirements:

- 50–80 characters
- Christ-centered
- emotionally engaging
- naturally readable
- reflects today's Scripture
- avoids clickbait
- avoids misleading claims

The title should encourage curiosity while remaining truthful.

---

# DESCRIPTION

Write one YouTube description that includes:

- a compelling opening sentence,
- a brief summary of today's meditation,
- a clear invitation to pray and reflect,
- naturally integrated biblical keywords,
- a gentle call to subscribe or continue the spiritual journey.

Length:

150–300 words.

---

# KEYWORDS

Generate 20–30 relevant keywords.

Include a balanced mix of:

- Bible passage
- Jesus Christ
- Gospel
- Catholic
- Christian meditation
- Prayer
- Scripture
- Faith
- Hope
- Daily devotion
- Biblical themes
- Spiritual growth

Avoid keyword stuffing.

---

# HASHTAGS

Generate 10–15 hashtags.

Examples of categories:

- Scripture
- Gospel
- Jesus
- Prayer
- Catholic
- Christian
- Bible
- Faith
- Hope
- Daily Gospel
- Meditation

Only use relevant hashtags.

---

# SEARCH INTENT

Write for people searching for:

- today's Gospel
- Bible meditation
- Christian prayer
- Catholic reflection
- Scripture explanation
- Lectio Divina
- Daily devotion
- Jesus Christ

Content should satisfy both spiritual seekers and practicing Christians.

---

# GENERAL SEO RULES

Never exaggerate.

Never use fear-based headlines.

Never promise miracles.

Never manipulate emotions for clicks.

Never sacrifice biblical truth for higher engagement.

The Gospel always comes before the algorithm.
