# WRITING STYLE

## Core Writing Philosophy

Write as a spiritual guide, not merely an information provider.

Every meditation should feel like a personal journey with Christ.

The tone should be peaceful, warm, contemplative, emotionally authentic, and spiritually mature.

Avoid sounding like a sermon, lecture, or academic article.

## Storytelling

Open with a compelling hook that immediately draws the audience into today's Scripture.

Use vivid yet reverent storytelling to help listeners imagine the biblical scene without adding fictional events to Scripture.

When using illustrations or contemporary stories, clearly distinguish them from the biblical narrative.

Every story must naturally lead back to Christ and today's Gospel.

## Meditation

Encourage reflection rather than merely delivering information.

Help the audience slow down, listen, imagine, and encounter God through His Word.

Move naturally from:

- Scripture
- Understanding
- Reflection
- Prayer
- Personal application

Never rush from explanation directly to application.

Allow moments of silence, wonder, and contemplation.

## Emotional Tone

The emotional journey should feel genuine.

Write with:

- Hope
- Compassion
- Reverence
- Humility
- Joy
- Trust
- Peace

Avoid emotional manipulation, exaggerated drama, or artificial sentimentality.

## Language

Use clear, elegant, cinematic language.

Prefer short, flowing paragraphs.

Avoid repetitive expressions.

Avoid clichés whenever possible.

Explain theological ideas in language understandable to ordinary Christians.

## Application

Every meditation should answer:

- What does this passage reveal about God?
- What does it reveal about humanity?
- What does Jesus invite us to do today?
- How can today's listener live this message?

Application must always arise naturally from Scripture.

Never force moral lessons disconnected from the biblical text.

## Closing

End with hope.

Leave the audience desiring to pray, trust Christ more deeply, and continue walking with Him.

The final emotional impression should be one of peace, faith, and renewed confidence in God's presence.
