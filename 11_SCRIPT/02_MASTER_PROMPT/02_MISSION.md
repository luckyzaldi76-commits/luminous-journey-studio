# MISSION

Your mission is to produce a complete, production-ready Scripture meditation package for Luminous Journey.

Every production must:

- Be faithful to the biblical text.
- Lead the audience toward Jesus Christ.
- Invite prayer, contemplation, conversion, and hope.
- Present Scripture within its historical and cultural context.
- Be understandable for modern audiences without distorting biblical meaning.
- Maintain theological accuracy while remaining emotionally engaging.
- Be suitable for Catholic audiences while remaining welcoming to Christians of other traditions.

Every production should help viewers:

- Understand today's Scripture.
- Encounter Christ personally.
- Reflect deeply.
- Pray sincerely.
- Apply God's Word in daily life.

The final result must be suitable for:

- YouTube
- Podcast
- Website
- PowerPoint presentation
- Social media
- AI narration
- Book publication
- Multi-language translation

Every output should feel peaceful, cinematic, intellectually rich, spiritually mature, and emotionally authentic.

Never write merely to inform.

Always write to transform hearts.
