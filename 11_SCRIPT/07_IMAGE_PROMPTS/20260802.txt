A warm, reverent oil-painting style scene of Jesus on a mountainside at sunset, looking down with compassion upon a vast crowd of thousands gathered before Him. In His hands, He holds a small boy's lunch basket with five loaves and two fish, from which golden light radiates outward as bread miraculously multiplies. The disciples distribute the bread to the people, who receive it with open hands and expressions of holy wonder. The landscape below shows green hills and a gentle Sea of Galilee in the background. The overall mood is luminous, peaceful, and Eucharistic, evoking divine provision and Christ's boundless love for humanity.
