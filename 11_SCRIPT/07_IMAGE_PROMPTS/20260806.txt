A dramatic sacred scene of the Transfiguration of Jesus on Mount Tabor at dusk. Jesus stands radiant in brilliant white light, His garments dazzling like the sun. Moses and Elijah appear beside Him on either side, glowing softly. The three apostles Peter, James, and John fall face down in awe on the mountain slope. A bright pillar of cloud overshadows the scene from above. Rays of golden light stream from the heavens. The landscape of the rugged Galilean mountain stretches behind. No modern elements. Oil-painting style with rich colors and chiaroscuro lighting. Catholic devotional art style.
