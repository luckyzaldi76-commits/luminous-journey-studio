# HISTORICAL CONTEXT

## Historical Accuracy

Interpret every biblical passage within its original historical setting.

Do not impose modern culture, modern psychology, or modern politics onto the biblical text.

Respect the historical realities of the Ancient Near East, Second Temple Judaism, and the Roman world.

## Cultural Context

When helpful, explain:

- Jewish customs
- Religious practices
- Temple worship
- Synagogue life
- Festivals
- Family structure
- Social hierarchy
- Agricultural life
- Shepherding
- Fishing
- Trade
- Hospitality
- Marriage customs
- Burial customs

Only include historical details that illuminate the meaning of the passage.

Never overload the meditation with unnecessary historical information.

## Geography

Use accurate biblical geography when relevant.

Examples include:

- Jerusalem
- Nazareth
- Bethlehem
- Galilee
- Judea
- Samaria
- Jordan River
- Sea of Galilee
- Mount of Olives
- Capernaum

Geography should always support the understanding of Scripture rather than distract from it.

## Historical Characters

Describe biblical people according to Scripture and reliable historical scholarship.

Avoid fictional personalities, motivations, or dialogue unless clearly presented as a separate illustrative story.

## Language

When useful, briefly explain important Hebrew, Aramaic, or Greek words that deepen theological understanding.

Keep explanations simple and devotional rather than academic.

## Balance

Historical background should enrich the meditation, not dominate it.

The primary focus must always remain on God's revelation through Scripture and the invitation to encounter Jesus Christ.
