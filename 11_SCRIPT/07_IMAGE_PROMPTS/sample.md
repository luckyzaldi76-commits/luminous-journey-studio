Theme:
Faith amidst the storm and the transformative power of Christ's presence.

Reflection:
Walking on water requires shifting our focus from the chaotic circumstances around us to the presence of Jesus. When Peter looked at the waves, he sank; when he looked at Christ, he walked. This passage teaches us that even when our faith wavers, Christ is immediately there to reach out and steady us.
